# Superstore Sales Analysis · Tableau

Interactive sales dashboard analyzing 9,994 orders across product 
categories, US states, and customer segments.

## Tools
Tableau Desktop

## Visualizations
- Monthly Sales & Profit trend (dual axis line chart)
- Profit by US State (filled map)
- Sales by Category & Sub-Category (treemap)
- Top 10 Products by Sales (bar chart)
- Sales by Customer Segment (bar chart)

## Key Insights
- Technology drives highest profit margins
- Texas and Illinois show consistent losses despite high sales volume
- Consumer segment represents over 50% of total sales
- Q4 consistently outperforms other quarters across all years
